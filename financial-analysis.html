What this dashboard does
💰
Financial overview

The report provides an instant snapshot of total revenue, profit, and quantity sold — with clear comparisons against prior year figures and variance indicators to highlight what's changed.

🌍
Geographic breakdown

Profit margins and sales performance are broken down by country, allowing immediate identification of top and underperforming markets at a glance.

📈
Trend analysis

Sales and profit trend lines reveal seasonality and growth patterns over time, enabling data-driven forecasting and strategic planning.

Key features
KPI cards with YoY comparison — Key metrics shown alongside prior year values with percentage change, giving immediate context to every number.
Profit margin by country & segment — Drill-down views reveal which markets and customer segments are most and least profitable.
Sales amount over time — Time series visualisations expose seasonal trends and long-term growth or decline patterns.
DAX measures — Custom DAX calculations power all metrics including dynamic YoY comparisons, running totals, and margin calculations.
