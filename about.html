My story

2015 — 2020

Linguistic High School & Language Immersion

A passion for travel and languages led me to a Linguistic High School, where I took part in immersive language trips across Europe and the US — Munich, Regensburg, Nice, Paris, and Orlando. Living with host families and attending language courses gave me a deep appreciation for different cultures and sharpened my ability to communicate across contexts.

2020 — 2023

Bachelor's in Tourism Management · Netherlands

I moved to the Netherlands to pursue a Bachelor's degree in Tourism Management — a natural extension of my love for travel and how industries work. Working on real-life projects with tourism companies and collaborating in international teams were formative experiences. In my second year, I completed an internship in Stockholm, Sweden, where I discovered the concept of slow tourism and developed a lasting curiosity for Nordic culture.

International experience

2023 — 2024

Master's in Business Intelligence

During my business studies, a Business Intelligence exam changed everything. I immediately recognized the potential of data and decided to pursue a Master's specialisation in BI. Every step of the program was engaging — the challenge of bringing data to life was something I found genuinely satisfying. After graduating, I continued building projects independently, several of which you can find in my portfolio.
