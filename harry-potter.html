What this project does
✨
The concept

Each Hogwarts house has a distinct personality — bold, cunning, intellectual, loyal. So do coffee beans. This project maps those personality traits to real coffee variety data, turning a whimsical analogy into a data-backed narrative.

📊
The data

Two Kaggle datasets were merged: one containing Harry Potter house characteristics and one with detailed coffee bean tasting profiles — acidity, body, aroma, flavour notes. The overlap between descriptive attributes drove the matching logic.

🎨
The design

The visualisation is built as a guided infographic — users journey through each house and its matched coffee, with custom colour palettes, house-themed styling, and interactive tooltips revealing detailed tasting notes and variety profiles.

The house matchings
🦁
Gryffindor
Bold, brave, passionate. Matched to a high-acidity, full-bodied coffee with intense flavour — a bean that makes an immediate, confident impression.
🐍
Slytherin
Ambitious, complex, refined. Matched to a dark, smooth roast with earthy undertones — sophisticated and not immediately obvious.
🦅
Ravenclaw
Intellectual, nuanced, precise. Matched to a light roast with delicate floral and citrus notes — complex enough to reward careful attention.
🦡
Hufflepuff
Warm, reliable, approachable. Matched to a balanced medium roast with nutty, caramel notes — comforting and universally enjoyable.
