What this dashboard does
🛒
Retail & products

Sales by point of sale and by product, revenue vs target, average discount and pieces sold — with a geographic view of stores across Italy.

🌱
ESG & sustainability

Environmental indicators — water, energy and fuel consumption, CO₂ emissions and waste recycling rates broken down by site, with an annual recycling KPI.

👷
People & safety

Headcount, employee satisfaction, training hours, accidents and injury rate — plus individual employee scorecards with performance and cost.

Key features
Retail & product performance — Revenue by point of sale and product with revenue-vs-target, discounts and pieces sold, mapped geographically.
ESG environmental indicators — Water, energy and fuel consumption, CO₂ emissions and waste-recycling rates per site, with a 72% annual recycling KPI.
HR & workplace safety — Headcount, satisfaction, training hours, accidents and injury rate, down to individual employee scorecards.
Finance & treasury — Credit-line exposure (Centrale Rischi) by institute and category, plus cash-flow, cumulative cash flow and reserves.
